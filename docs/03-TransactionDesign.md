# Transaction Design

## Transactions

### CreatePurchaseOrder

Creates a new purchase order.

---

### ApprovePurchaseOrder

Approves a purchase order.

---

### RejectPurchaseOrder

Rejects a purchase order.

---

### ShipPurchaseOrder

Marks order as shipped.

---

### ReceivePurchaseOrder

Marks order as received.

---

### ReadPurchaseOrder

Returns purchase order details.

---

### GetAllPurchaseOrders

Returns all purchase orders.
